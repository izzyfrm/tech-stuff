# What AIs To Use

A quick guide for picking the right AI.

---

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/izzyfrm/tech-stuff/main/emojis/claude-ai.svg" width="22" alt="Claude logo"> [Claude AI](https://claude.ai)
**Free**  
Best for coding and writing documents.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/izzyfrm/tech-stuff/main/emojis/claude-code-mascot.svg" width="22" alt="Claude Code logo"> [Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-code)
**$17/month**  
Best for serious coding and larger projects.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/izzyfrm/tech-stuff/main/emojis/chatgpt.svg" width="22" alt="ChatGPT logo"> [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com)
**Free**  
Good for daily tasks and general questions.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/izzyfrm/tech-stuff/main/emojis/codex.svg" width="22" alt="Codex logo"> [Codex](https://openai.com/codex/)
**$20/month**  
Good for coding and debugging.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/izzyfrm/tech-stuff/main/emojis/gemini.svg" width="22" alt="Gemini logo"> [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com)
**Free**  
Good for normal tasks and questions.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/izzyfrm/tech-stuff/main/emojis/kimi-ai.svg" width="22" alt="Kimi logo"> [Kimi](https://www.kimi.com)
**Free / Limited**  
Very good for larger tasks.

### <img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=dianoetic.tech&sz=128" width="22" alt="Dianoetic AI logo"> [Dianoetic AI](https://dianoetic.tech)
Good for C# coding.

---

> Use the AI that fits the task. You do not have to stick with only one.
